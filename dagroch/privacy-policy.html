Overview

Thirty3rdStudio ("we", "us", or "our") built Daily Growth Challenge as a mobile application. This page describes our privacy practices.

The short version: Daily Growth Challenge does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

Data Collection

We do not collect any data. Daily Growth Challenge does not transmit any information to us or any third-party server. There are no accounts, no sign-ins, no analytics, no tracking, and no crash reporting.

All content you create or interact with in Daily Growth Challenge exists solely on your device.

Data Retention

Because no data is collected, there is nothing for us to retain. Deleting the app from your device removes all associated data permanently.

Third-Party Services

Daily Growth Challenge is distributed through the Apple App Store. Apple may collect certain data as part of their platform (downloads, purchase records). This is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy and is outside our control.

We do not integrate any advertising SDKs, analytics platforms, or social media trackers.

Children's Privacy

Daily Growth Challenge does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change our privacy practices, we will update this page and revise the effective date above.
